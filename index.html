The exchange economy, alive.

BlockTown turns exchange activity into a living 3D neighborhood. Every storefront represents a market, with queues and visitors scaled to its reported trading volume.

Live data: centralized exchange volumes come from CoinGecko; decentralized exchange activity comes from DefiLlama. Fresh responses are marked LIVE. Source timestamps appear below; the most recent saved data keeps the town browsing smoothly between updates.

Explore: search names or networks, filter by exchange type, and save favorites. Choose a store, drag to orbit, scroll or pinch to zoom, choose the weather and time of day, and use the playback controls to change timelapse speed and the macro lens.
